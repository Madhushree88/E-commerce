
# Order Cancellation Policy

Customers can cancel an order before it has been shipped by submitting a cancellation request through their account.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is no longer available, and the customer must follow the applicable return policy after delivery.

For prepaid orders cancelled before shipment, refunds are initiated to the original payment method within 2 business days of cancellation approval.

Cash on Delivery orders cancelled before shipment do not require a refund because no payment has been collected.

If a cancellation request fails or the order status is unclear, the customer should contact the support team with their order ID.
