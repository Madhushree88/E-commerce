
# Cash on Delivery (COD) Refund Policy

This is a fictional policy created for the Nykaa capstone project.

For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are processed through bank transfer after the returned item passes quality inspection.

Customers must provide valid bank account details through the approved secure refund process.

Once approved, a COD refund normally takes 5 to 7 business days to reach the customer's bank account.

If the refund has not arrived after 7 business days, the customer can contact support using their order ID.
