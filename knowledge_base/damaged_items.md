
# Damaged Item Claim Process

Customers who receive a damaged or defective product must report the issue within 48 hours of delivery.

The customer must provide a valid order ID, a description of the damage, and clear photographs of the affected product.

The support team reviews the claim and may request additional information before approving a replacement or refund.

Approved damaged-item claims are eligible for a replacement, subject to stock availability, or a refund through the original payment method.

If the claim is rejected or remains unresolved for more than 5 business days, the customer may request escalation to a senior support representative.
