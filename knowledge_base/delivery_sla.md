
# Delivery Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This is a fictional delivery policy for the Nykaa capstone project.

Standard delivery orders are expected to arrive within 3 to 7 business days after dispatch.

Orders shipped to remote locations may take 7 to 10 business days.

Customers can track their shipments using the order ID provided in their account.

If an order exceeds its expected delivery window, the customer can contact support for investigation and escalation.
