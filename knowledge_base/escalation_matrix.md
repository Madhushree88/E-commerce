
# Customer Support Escalation Matrix

This is a fictional customer-support escalation policy created for the Nykaa capstone project.

Level 1 support handles routine order-status questions, delivery tracking, cancellation requests, and general policy inquiries.

Level 2 support handles unresolved refund delays, disputed return decisions, damaged-item claims, and repeated delivery failures.

Level 3 support handles suspected fraud, serious privacy incidents, and complaints that remain unresolved after Level 2 review.

A complaint should be escalated to Level 2 if Level 1 cannot resolve it within 2 business days.

Critical privacy or security incidents must be escalated directly to Level 3 without waiting for the normal escalation timeline.
