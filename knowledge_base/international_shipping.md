
# International Shipping Restrictions

International shipping is available only to selected countries and eligible delivery addresses under this fictional capstone policy.

Beauty products containing restricted liquids, aerosols, or flammable ingredients cannot be shipped internationally.

Electronics containing certain lithium batteries may be restricted based on destination-country regulations.

International delivery timelines vary by destination, customs clearance, and courier availability.

Customers may be responsible for applicable customs duties and import taxes.

If an international shipment is restricted or rejected by customs, the customer should contact support for assistance with the available resolution options.
