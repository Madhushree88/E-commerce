
# Loyalty Points Redemption Policy

This is a fictional loyalty-points policy created for the Nykaa capstone project.

Customers earn 1 loyalty point for every INR 100 spent on eligible purchases.

Each loyalty point is worth INR 1 when redeemed against eligible future orders.

Customers can redeem a maximum of 500 loyalty points per order.

Loyalty points cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to another customer.

If an order is cancelled or returned, the loyalty points earned from that order are reversed after the refund is processed.
