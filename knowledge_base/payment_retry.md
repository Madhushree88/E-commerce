
# Payment Failure and Retry Policy

If a payment fails during checkout, customers should check their payment method and transaction status before retrying.

Customers may retry a failed payment up to three times within 30 minutes.

If money is deducted but the order is not confirmed, the transaction is reviewed and any eligible reversal is initiated within 3 to 5 business days.

Customers should not repeatedly pay for the same order while a previous transaction is pending.

If a payment issue remains unresolved after 5 business days, the customer should contact support with the order ID and payment reference number.
