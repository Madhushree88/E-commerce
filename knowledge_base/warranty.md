
# Warranty Terms by Product Category

This is a fictional warranty policy created for the Nykaa capstone project.

Electronics products include a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.

Home products include a 6-month limited warranty for eligible manufacturing defects.

Apparel and Footwear products do not carry a standard warranty, but eligible items may be returned within their applicable return windows.

Beauty products do not have a standard warranty, although damaged or defective items may qualify for a claim under the damaged-item policy.

Warranty coverage excludes accidental damage, misuse, and normal wear and tear.
